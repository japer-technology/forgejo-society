# Archive

Modules that are not part of the active Forgejo Intelligence runtime.

- `github-only/` — capabilities that depend on GitHub-only platform features.
  Kept for design history. See
  `.forgejo-intelligence/help/unsupported-github-surfaces.md`.
- `deferred/` — capabilities postponed until they are specified,
  fixture-backed, and reviewed.

Nothing under `archive/` is discovered by the orchestrator. Presence here is
not permission.
